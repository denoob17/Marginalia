How We Choose an Excerpt

  1. One of us underlines a line while reading — on paper, not a highlighter app.
  2. We read it out loud to each other before deciding it's worth keeping.
  3. We each write a short, separate reflection without reading the other's first.
  4. We compare notes and publish both reactions side by side.

“All happy families are alike.”

— Leo Tolstoy, Anna Karenina

The most quoted opening line on this entire list, and we still think it's underrated. One sentence and you already know the whole book will argue with itself.

“It was the best of times, it was the worst of times.”
